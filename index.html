Our Mission

About AAaogyamAi

AAaogyamAi is a clinical decision-support platform engineered to turn cryptic laboratory values into clear, actionable medical intelligence. We empower patients and clinicians to interpret CBC, metabolic, lipid, thyroid, and comprehensive diagnostic panels through calibrated biomarker range meters, longitudinal trajectory tracking, and secure doctor consults.

Our interdisciplinary team bridges pathology informatics, hematology expertise, and responsible AI engineering. All analytical models are dual-validated across 2.4 million de-identified clinical lab records and designed to support—never replace—licensed clinical judgment.

Key Platform Pillars

  • Clear Visual Diagnostics: Translate cryptic lab codes and reference ranges into intuitive, color-calibrated biomarker meters.
  • Trajectory Tracking: Monitor biomarker velocities and surface subtle physiologic drift over 30, 90, and 365-day horizons.
  • Virtual Exam Rooms: Connect patients with certified hematologists and physicians in HIPAA-compliant tele-consultations.
  • Laboratory-Grade Standards: Built around CLIA-oriented workflows, CAP validation guidelines, and native HL7 / FHIR data interoperability.

Data Protection & HIPAA

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AAaogyamAi collects, uses, encrypts, and protects your personal identification and protected health information (PHI) across our website and clinical portals.

1. Information We Collect

We collect patient profile identifiers (e.g., name, date of birth, contact details), uploaded clinical laboratory reports (PDFs, DICOMs, image scans), parsed biomarker telemetry, usage interactions, and technical security audit logs necessary to safeguard your records.

2. How We Use Health Information

Your data is utilized strictly to parse lab reports into structured biomarker metrics, generate AI diagnostic insights, track historical health trends, facilitate telehealth appointments with verified physicians, and satisfy legal audit obligations.

3. Military-Grade Security Safeguards

We implement end-to-end AES-256 encryption for data at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption for data in transit. Access to sensitive PHI is governed by least-privilege role-based access control (RBAC), multi-factor authentication, and continuous security auditing under HIPAA Business Associate standards.

4. Zero Unauthorized Sharing

We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal health information. Data is disclosed only to clinicians you explicitly select for consultations, authorized laboratory partners with signed BAAs, or when mandated by binding judicial orders.

5. Patient Rights & DPDP 2023 Compliance

In full compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and global health data frameworks, you retain complete rights to inspect, download a copy of, rectify, or request permanent deletion of your stored health dossier at any time.

6. Data Retention Protocols

We maintain active records only for as long as your account remains active or as required by clinical continuity and regulatory retention statutes. You can request deletion directly through your profile settings.

7. Privacy Inquiries & Data Protection Officer

For any questions regarding your privacy rights or data handling practices, contact our Data Protection Office directly via the support module inside the Patient Profile portal.
